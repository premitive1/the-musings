Monuments to Our Dreams

In the course of collaborating with other members of the community and
perhaps in part due to empathy I find myself easily preoccuppied with
the goals of others, losing sight of those things which drove me to
certain courses of action. What a comfort and joy to revel in the
aspirations of those who, like me, see in the Bitcoin protocol a new
economic technology which vastly increases the utility of money. I get
carried away in the energy and excitement found in organizing with other
bitcoiners, but I should not lose sight of my own dreams. Nor should
you.

If I started Bitcoin Brunch as a ploy to get rich or famous, I failed
and it cost me hundreds of hours I can never respend. However, I do not
regret those hours because I did not set out to achieve such goals. I
wanted to meet people and advance important public discourse on the role
that money plays in the lives of human beings. I hoped that in
establishing relationships with other bitcoiners eventually we would
mutually find a way to use bitcoin as better money in our lives, as
opposed to relegating that to an aspiration for our progeny. Many of the
bitcoiners I met share this same goal, but our visions for achieving
them vary as much as any set of opinions that exist in society. We come
in so many colors, shapes, and sizes, that from some distance I find it
hard to believe we all voluntarily sit under the same banner of bitcoin.

Given these differences, working with other bitcoiners inevitably leads
to tension and hopefully a regular reevaluation of ones goals. In
choosing to build community on my free time, while otherwise leading a
normie life in the FIAT world, I put myself between a rock and a hard
place that actually relieves the pressure of having to decide which
direction to go. When my commitments to other efforts contradict my
goals, I feel it very succinctly because it interferes with my career.
Unlike others, I did not yet make bitcoin a career and the explanation
for why requires another ten columns or essays. I write all this because
I feel a great excitement in the wake of the Bitcoin Vortex, and expect
interesting things to happen as a result. I still cannot perceive the
extent to which I, or Bitcoin Brunch, will participate in these
movements, but I say unequivocally that I can't wait to see what
happens!
