Life is full of ideologies putting themselves as the default for all
decision making, regarding what's right and wrong, but you are the
default.

If you ever hear mention of the innocence of a child, know that it comes
from inexperience. From birth your family, friends, school, community,
and government all work tireless, though perhaps unconsciously, to
override that inexperience with their concepts, ideas, and ideologies.
Starting from the state of knowing nothing, you soon come to believe
many things about what other people think, and likely very little about
how your life works. The older you get the more importance ideology
plays in your life, with society constantly asking the population to
make ethical decisions with wide-reaching impacts. Should we accomodate
or oppose mass immigration into the USA? Should we provide powerful
weapons to Ukraine for their war with Russia? Should we concern
ourselves with the size of the public debt? These are some questions
that this group in particular may consider with regularity, but would
never occur to a young child. How did we go from that pure state of
curiosity to cynically toying with the lives of billions of people and
justifying it through the filter of ideology? What have we gained in
giving up our naivete?

The ability for an idea or a movement to be greater than any one person
is precisely a result of more than one person dedicating themselves to
the same purpose. It's immensity is only the aggregate of the various
individual wills that make it up. Just as the ideologies of previous
epochs have disappeared with the people who believed them, so too will
all those that we hold so dear today, as we inevitably succumb to time.
Our progeny will mutate, adapt, and transmogrify our ideas into concepts
we could barely recognize. Our names, if they're remembered, will be
attached to actions we never committed, and goals we never had. Rest
assured that it is out of your control. If you are the center of your
own existence, the source from which all your relationships with the
universe emerge, why should you hold the constructs of other people
preeminently over your own feelings?

The heterodox school of Austrian economics promotes the importance of
methodological individualism, which is an overt emphasis that all
actions are ultimately performed by individuals, not collectives. All
the monumental protagonists of various ideological movements throughout
recorded history are remembered for what they did as individuals. You
are always the main character of your own narrative and as long as you
submissively adopt the chains of ideology you will never rise to be the
hero of your story.

Don't take these words as me trying to impose my own rules upon you.
Believe whatever you want, but for the sake of your happiness and the
quality of your life, make sure that you've considered these mental
interior decorations thoroughly--that you accept them voluntarily and
consciously. Not as a result of life happening to you, but because you
lived your life fully in control of what you did and thought. Don't let
this wild world confuse you into thinking you're a minor character in a
bigger picture. You were always the star. You are the default.
