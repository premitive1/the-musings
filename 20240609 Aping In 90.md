Aping In

*What is aping in?* Mostly the product of FOMO, (fear of missing out)
aping in is when someone reverts to simian short-sightedness as they
seek profitable yields in cryptocoin speculations

We often discuss the fear of missing out, but do not spend nearly so
much time exploring a sentiment better capable of driving behavior,
which is the fear of losing everything. In the realm of cryptocurrencies
and price-speculative investing we find exploits, rug pulls, and scams
so prevalent that the mere mention of crypto sooner elicits thoughts of
risk than of any potentially beneficial opportunities. I find it no
wonder that college graduates would boo at the mere mention of Bitcoin.
These same people can little differentiate between Bitcoin and the
plethora of cheap parking lot knock-offs that have multiplied like
cockroaches for the past several years. How could I expect someone with
that mindset to ever dive more than toe deep into the ocean of
understanding bitcoin which, seemingly unique from other digital
currency projects, exists to address fundamental flaws in the structure
of our economy.

Of these people though, a portion do have price-speculative investments
and want exposure to bitcoin in such a way that they can hedge their
bets, gaining something regardless of whether the dollar-price goes up
or down. Moderately sophisticated retail traders brought this up to me
as a reason they hadn't put bitcoin into their portfolios. They want to
hedge their bets. Therefore I see the approval of an ETF as how
nocoiners will expose themselves to bitcoin. Despite our sincerest
desires for custodial self-sovereignty of money, the reality for most in
the west is one of constant comfort and resigned complacency. This fiat
mentality renders one incapable of critically analyzing the
contradictions in society, especially those which underly our standards
of living. The very catastrophe they fear in bitcoin is precisely that
which they fail to prepare for in the dollar: a crisis of value. When
the public loses confidence in the dollar it will throw our valuations
into calamity. We will no longer be able to enforce the virtual debits
and credits living in servers and displaying on computer screens. The
rich will find themselves impoverished. Precious things will be deemed
worthless.

To escape the adversity of scarcity we must be capable of bringing value
to our community. Our society has many histories and mythologies
involving people starting from scratch after catastrophe, be it man made
or an act of god. Do you instinctively understand the importance of
relationships and reputation? That's how people reestablish themselves
after financial ruin. They look deep within themselves to discover what
they can do to make the world better, and collaborate with those around
them to realize that value. Cultivate skills and talents that
circumstance can't steal from you, that make you a valuable neighbor
until the day you die. Then you don't have to fear losing everything, or
missing out on anything.
