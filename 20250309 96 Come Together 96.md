Bitcoiners should come together because there are so few of us and it's
such a crazy, isolating world we live in.

Politics, religion, sports, language, philosophy, music, art. These
powerful cultures simultaneously bring some together, while tearing
others apart. The war over money is no different. The power it has
within our lives ensures deep fissures between members of competing
ideologies, and make no mistake about it, money is an idea. An idea so
powerful it pervades our lives, and conquers a significant portion of
our time. An idea so divisive it imbues itself in all wars. Given such a
profound influence, how can bitcoiners navigate the volatile gulf
between scarcity and fiat? The printing presses can only defraud so much
of the world economy into bitcoin before breaking itself, at which point
true scarcity in the digital and physical landscapes must come into
harmony, or wreak havoc indefinitely.

Humans already obsess over mortality, and we’ve thought of a million
different ways our deaths could come about. How many stories of
armaggeddon do we know and tell? Will climate change bring about the end
of the world, or is rapture going to signal the return of Christ? One of
the main purposes of Bitcoin Brunch was to meet other bitcoiners and
have in person discussions at a time when everyone seemed comfortable in
online safe spaces. I want to learn about what inspires people and
motivates them to build with bitcoin despite all the crazy distractions.

Coming together, in this space, for this brief time, despite all the
forces and energies seeking to divide us, is a miracle. Rather than
focus on what separates us, we should maximize the magic created when
bitcoiners come together. When people combine their qualities, they
multiply the value within their community. We will move candlesticks
much more by connecting the existing dots than expending energy on cold
bitcoin adoption. We will overcome the friction of precoiners more
easily with examples of lives worth living, than convincing them with
rhetorical persuasion. We must inspire ourselves to live those lives, to
break off from what’s normal and tired, and create with our own efforts
and in our own lives what’s novel and inspiring. I believe this is
possible, we just need to come together.
