I abhor a discussion about the dollar price of bitcoin and
aspirationally advocate for the decoupling of value from fiat currency.
I dream of the world where we've separated money from central planners
such that governments can no longer disguise coercion with a printing
press. In the good times, they export violence to the edges of the
empire to maintain the appearance of normalcy and stability, but don't
be fooled, even the most freedom loving democracy cracks down on dissent
with an iron fist during the bad times. (See "The Miami Model"
documentary from 2003 as an example.) In March of last year I wrote,
"Before having the power to command, governments demand power through
violence." In other words, when the power of FIAT falters, government
physiologically reverts to a state of violence. Therefore I imagine an
inevitable future in which the governments, dependent on their own FIAT
currencies, and losing their grasp on command of value, will import
violent authoritarianism to keep the system going as long as possible.
At some point in this process we have to come to terms with the
fundamental truth that absent the monopolization of violence, FIAT
currencies lose their economic utility. The dark magic of printing money
from nothing will not long withstand the eviscerating forces unleashed
by free markets.

Given all the weight and importance of money in our lives, what kind of
neurotic insanity do we subject ourselves to in waiting for
number-go-up. Waiting for the dollar price of Bitcoin to rise is like
waiting for a pool to evaporate from sunlight as you're drowning in it.
I could write this and worse about the evils of a faulty measuring
stick, but you need only look back on previous essays to find myriad
rants and railings against our broken monetary system. Yet even after
two hundreds weeks and countless hours (certainly more than 100!) I
cannot claim to have a mind devoid of preoccupation with the dollar
price of bitcoin. In the hands of such a volatile lover as the dollar,
the price of bitcoin can swing wildly in the blink of an eye, breaking
plans poorly made, destroying dreams falsely promised, and delaying the
harbinger of the inevitable. At some point along the slow road to zero,
fiat currencies become completely useless in an economy and people will
no longer use them as a measure of price.

Even with bitcoin as a new unit of account, any set back in the ability
for governments and banks to print money will have a world shaking
effect on world economy. Many businesses and business models we require
reconsideration or deletion. Many ways of making money today will have
no value tomorrow. Many will face their obsolescence, when life will
demand of them to either master something new or sink into despair.
Therefore number-go-up aspires to evade that inescapable demise. If the
bitcoin value can grow faster than my competence can shrink, I win.
Nirvana. This kind of winning is like a lottery that bankrupts its
winner. Rather, we should strive to indefinitely increase our value,
regardless of changing times, evolving technologies, and aging bodies.
It’s not a sin to want to have as much money as you can, and for it to
have as much value as possible, but never let how much you value money
rob you of your own personal worth. Don’t let your love of money hold
you back in your journey of growth and discovery. Have the courage to
fill your time with things you love to do, surrounded by people you care
for and respect. Show the strength because you have that strength. If
you are reading this. If you are human. If you are alive!
